In this study you watch educational videos while your webcam records your face. The webcam video is recorded and stored on a university server. It is used only to estimate where on the screen you look.

You are identified only by your participant code, never by your name. The retention period is short: the recording stays on this server until the analyses are done, then it is deleted.

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing the page, and you can ask for your data to be deleted: nago.daniel@student.ms.sapientia.ro.

By ticking the box below you agree to the recording.
