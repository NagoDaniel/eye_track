Coordinates are video-normalized (0 to 1). #}

AOI editor

Pick a moment with the slider, draw an area on the picture, then set when it exists (start and end, in seconds of video). Drag a shape to move it, drag its dots to reshape it. Shapes that do not exist at the chosen moment are dashed. The text box below holds exactly what is saved; press Save to keep it.
